Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

What Isla reads

When you sign in with Google, Isla asks for two read-only permissions. With Google Calendar access it reads the events on your calendars for today and tomorrow, such as their title, time, color and meeting link, and with Gmail metadata access it reads the sender, subject, date and labels of messages in your inbox. Isla cannot read the body or attachments of any email, and it cannot send, change or delete anything in your account.

Where your data goes

Nowhere outside your Mac. Isla talks directly to Google's APIs from your computer and shows the results in the notch, it has no server of its own, and it does not store, sell, share or transfer your Google data to anyone. The sign-in tokens Google issues are kept in your Mac's keychain, the events and messages Isla shows stay in memory and are gone when the app quits, and the only Google detail saved in its settings is which calendars you chose to hide.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Isla's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign out of an account from Isla's menu, which also revokes its token with Google, or remove Isla at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your Mac.
