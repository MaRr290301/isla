Isla

A small Mac app that turns the MacBook camera notch into a panel for your next meetings, new mail and files you want to keep at hand.

Hover the notch and it opens with your next Google Calendar meetings, your newest unread Gmail and a shelf for files you drag onto it, and when new mail arrives it peeks out of the notch for a few seconds, so you see who wrote without switching apps.

Isla is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's Mac, it reads your calendar and your unread mail, showing a message's text only when you hover it for a preview, and it never sends any of that anywhere else.
